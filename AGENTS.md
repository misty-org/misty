# UI conventions

- Shared dropdown and context-menu selections use checkmarks, never dot indicators. Collection toolbars must not add a filter menu that duplicates their visible section tabs.
- Use the approved black, white and gray palette. Do not introduce green, sage, colored status dots, tinted selections or accent-colored focus rings unless the user explicitly requests color for that surface. Convey status through text and icons as well as contrast.
- Exception: item-type icon glyphs on content surfaces (the file explorer, and collection rows and cards on Spaces and other collection pages) use the shared tones in `src/shared/ui/icons/itemTones.ts`. Color the glyph only; icon tiles, hover, selection, focus and status stay monochrome. Tones mark what an item is, never its state. The tab strip, all sidebars (including the Extensions rail) and browser chrome stay uncolored. Add new kinds to the shared map rather than picking colors locally.
- Exception: tab groups use their chosen color: the filled label chip, the outlines of the group's tabs and the editor's swatches. Hover, focus and the rest of the tab strip stay monochrome, and a selected swatch shows a checkmark.
- Exception: each Space has a color (`src/features/workspace/windowColors.ts`), and a window takes the color of the Space it shows now: a quiet wash over the window's chrome (the frame and tab strip), changing when the window switches Space, and the swatch in the Space menu's Color list. Page content, hover, focus and selection stay monochrome, and the selected color shows a checkmark. A Space is given a color before any window shows it. Incognito windows show their Space's color too.
- Exception: official brand logos for connected apps (Integrations rows and tiles, and the app chips under the Agents composer), for coding tools in the composer's Code menu (via `CodingToolIcon`) and for source browsers in the browser import flow (onboarding, Settings and the Bookmarks page) keep their original artwork via `BrandIcon` from `src/shared/ui/icons/brand-icons`. Never recolor them or hand-draw a substitute; apps without artwork fall back to a monochrome Lucide icon. Status around them stays text and icons.
- Exception: status dots on agent conversation rows (the Agents sidebar Recents) follow Claude's session list: gray at rest, pulsing while queued or working, amber when the run needs the user, red when it failed, using the `--misty-status-*` tokens in `src/styles/App.css`. A settled run's dot is filled until its conversation is read on this device (chats live on the device that started them, `docs/design/agent-chats-local/BRIEF.md`) and a hollow gray ring afterwards, meaning nothing new. Every dot keeps a text label (tooltip and screen reader). Do not spread status color to other surfaces.
- Exception: the night light (Settings › Appearance › Night light, `src/features/settings/store/nightLight.ts`) lays one warm multiply tint over the whole window and every browser page while it is on. It is off by default, the only setting that schedules anything visual, and the only place that warmth may appear. The theme itself stays Dark, Light or System.
- Exception: diffs (`src/features/agents/components/changes/`) tint added lines green and removed lines red, like Claude Code and Codex, with matching +/− counts. The tint marks the line's change, never state; file headers, buttons, selection and focus stay monochrome, and other surfaces don't borrow these colors.
- Exception: agent conversations (`src/features/agents/`, the control island, the cursor companion panel) follow `docs/design/agent-chat/`. Every list of steps (checklist, sub-agents, plan, app and screen requests, unfinished work) is the shared `StatusTable` from `progress/StatusTable.tsx`: a status icon, a status word, the thing itself and a short result. Working text shimmers. Status icons are allowed; amber marks "waiting for you", red marks "failed" and a soft green marks "done" (`--misty-status-*`), always with the word beside them. Connected apps show their `BrandIcon` logo. Actions are real buttons (primary for the one that moves things forward), never underlined links. The final answer is plain formatted text in the transcript, never a banner, toast or error popup; a run that did not finish shows its answer and a "Not finished" table, and a failure shows as a table row where it happened.
- Spaces follow `docs/design/spaces-simplified/BRIEF.md` and `docs/design/spaces-as-profiles/BRIEF.md`: an owner and members with no roles or permissions, agents listed under their person. A window shows one Space at a time and switches in place. The Space's controls are the Space row in the Space bar above the pages (`SpaceRow` in `src/features/spaces/components/spaceRow/`, `SpaceBar` in `src/app/layouts/DesktopLayout/`): the Space's avatar and name with its menu (Spaces with a checkmark, Open in new window, New Space, Rename, Color, Sites that open here, Leave or Delete), then Home, Chat, People and the Memory icon (Lucide `Brain`). The Memory icon opens the Space's memory tree; Start capture and Stop capture are a button inside the tree, at its left. The Space bar can auto-hide (Settings › Appearance). There is no Space settings page and no bar inside Space pages; nothing else adds rails, pills, launchers or a ⌘K palette. The Space home is the gallery with a "Create" row in gray; there are no pins or favorites. The address bar only opens the shared ⌘T panel, and panel rows show the target, never a verb.
- Browsing never needs an account (`docs/design/no-account-browsing/BRIEF.md`). Sign-in is asked only for sync, Spaces and agents, through the shared sign-in sheet over the window (`SignInSheet` in `src/features/auth/signInSheet/`), whose title names the reason; nothing prompts on a timer. A signed-out window shows no Space: no Space bar and no Space color, and the sidebar's account row reads Sign in. The bar appears once the person signs in. Signing out keeps browsing on the device.
- Teach mode (`src/features/agents/lessons/`, `docs/design/agents-at-cursor/BRIEF.md` revision 4) is a conversation mode beside Ask, Auto and Plan and a companion switch. Its turns run the tutor's fast lane, never the agent loop. Lessons have their own section in the agent sidebar under a plain "Lessons" header, rows hanging off it with `TreeBranch`, status and progress as text. A lesson's objectives use the shared `StatusTable`; a course is a row with its lessons hanging off it. The companion's focus outline is monochrome.
- Companion drawings (`src/features/agents/companion/companionSketch.ts`) stay monochrome: the whiteboard is a white card with near-black hand-drawn strokes in both themes, and screen marks are white strokes with a dark shadow. They appear only when the person asks for a drawing, and the transcript shows the drawing itself, never its source.
- The memory tree (`src/features/memory-tree/`, the Space section `memory`) is a React Flow canvas of what a Space remembers: Space, branches (Moments, Summaries, Facts, Workflows), groups, then single memories. It has no page title and no pipeline or health strip; search, a Today/Week/All window, a checkmarked Filter menu and Refresh sit in one toolbar, and a details panel on the right is where a node is forgotten, after a count. Glyph tones come from `itemTones` (`memory*` kinds); nodes, edges and selection stay monochrome. Screen capture (`src/features/screen-memory/`) is always a session the person starts and stops, never ambient, and no agent tool starts it.
- Tabs always sit in the tab sidebar, on the left or the right of the window (`src/features/app-shell/dockingLayout.ts`). There is no horizontal tab strip: do not add top or bottom tab positions, per-pane toolbar rows or layout code that branches on tab orientation. Layouts saved with tabs on the top or bottom open with tabs on the left. Focus mode hides the sidebar and the Space bar; a browser page then shows its own toolbar row.
- Exception: the agent browser beside a chat (`src/features/agents/agentBrowser/`) is a small browser of its own with a horizontal tab strip above its toolbar row and page. The window's tabs stay in the sidebar.
- Nested rows (a thread under its agent, anything new) hang off their parent with the shared `TreeBranch` from `@/shared/ui` (`NavigationTree`), the thin muted trunk with ticks and a small rounded last corner from the old navbar's app tree. Don't draw indent lines or borders locally. Vertical tab groups use it too, in the group's color.
- Never show "Loading…" or "Preparing …" text. First loads render animated skeletons shaped like the content that replaces them (`Skeleton`, `SkeletonList`, `SkeletonDocument`, `CollectionSkeleton` from `@/shared/ui`). Background revalidation keeps the current content on screen; never blank a surface or drop to a skeleton while it runs. A Refresh the person presses (a refresh button) is the exception: the content shows skeletons shaped like it until the reload finishes.
- The settings sidebar follows Claude's settings: the Search field, then pages under plain muted group labels named by feature (Settings, Browser, Agents), each row a Lucide icon and the page name, the selected row a filled gray pill. No dividers, Account footer, nested disclosures, tab strips or chevrons on settings buttons (sidebar entries and rows alike). Each page opens one scrolling page with no title bar; plain section headers carry it. Account, plan and billing live on the website; settings only link there (General › About).
- `src/features/settings/settingsRegistry.tsx` is the single settings navigation registry. Use `DesktopSettingsSection`, `DesktopSettingsRow` and `SettingsControls` for settings UI. Page names are one word that never repeats or echoes its group's name, with no ampersands; each page has a unique icon. Keep the sidebar to about a dozen pages: add a section to a page before adding a page.
- Settings pages are plain lists: no card boxes, hairlines between rows, every control right-aligned to one shared edge. Rows and sections carry a short muted line of helper text that explains what the setting does, its scope or its privacy and destructive consequences; keep it to a sentence or two and never repeat the label. Reset appears only while a value differs from its default and never moves the control. Up to four short choices use a segmented control; longer lists use a checkmarked dropdown, never a stack of radio buttons. Failures show as an inline notice in plain language, never raw setting keys or server errors.
- Settings belong to the device: they are saved in `misty.db` and never sync or reach the server. Do not add account-synced settings, outboxes, or "Settings sync" surfaces. Server-owned resources (conversation retention, hosted Misty, the model catalog) keep their own APIs and are not settings. Device identity and OS keychain secrets stay distinct from preferences.
- Approved Sync direction: `docs/design/sync/BRIEF.md`, revision 2, including the approved monochrome palette. Its server-account settings model is superseded: settings are device-local. Historical mockup screenshots do not override these instructions.

# Commits

- Commit messages and pull request descriptions never carry a `Co-Authored-By:` trailer or any other AI attribution line ("Generated with ...", tool or model names). Commits are authored by the person alone.
