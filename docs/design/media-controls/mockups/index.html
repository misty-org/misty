Media controls in the rail

The navigator stays the thin 62px icon rail. While anything plays, one artwork tile sits at the bottom of the rail, above your profile. A ring around it shows progress. The full player opens beside the rail, never inside it. Tabs that make sound get a speaker you can click to mute.

1 · Playing, at rest

A tile, nothing more

The page's own artwork, with a thin ring for progress. When nothing is playing, the tile isn't there.

2 · Hover

Play/pause in one click

Hovering shows a pause or play glyph over the art. Clicking the tile toggles playback without opening anything. A tooltip names the tab.
