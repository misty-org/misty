Agent window: take over, detach and dock

When an agent needs the whole app (a canvas, pasting an image, dragging between apps), it takes over a Misty window. Detach gives you a fresh window and docks the agent's window small in a corner, like a video in picture in picture on iPhone. The agent keeps working in it. Pick a state.

How the docked window behaves
  • It is the agent's real window, still laid out at full size. The page is drawn small, so the page doesn't switch to a mobile layout and the agent's own captures stay sharp.
  • Drag it anywhere; let go and it snaps to the nearest corner. Fling it toward a side edge and it floats there, still whole and on screen.
  • Click it to open the agent's window at full size. Dock on the island shrinks it back.
  • Hover shows Open and Stop. Stop ends the task, the same as Stop on the island.
  • Several takeovers overlap into one fanned stack, up to five; more show as a count on the stack. Clicking the stack opens a carousel to pick one, with the picked agent's current step underneath.
